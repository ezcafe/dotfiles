# Pattern Library (Condensed)

## Impulse (1-2-3-4-5)

- Motive sequence in trend direction.
- Apply three hard impulse rules first.
- Typical psychology:
  - 1: early transition, often doubted
  - 2: deep pullback, old-trend bias
  - 3: strongest recognition and momentum
  - 4: complex/sideways pause
  - 5: final push, often divergence risk

## ZigZag (A-B-C, 5-3-5)

- Usually sharp correction.
- Often appears as Wave 2 or in combinations.
- Common C vs A relation near 1.0 (also 0.618/1.618 cases).

## Flat Family (A-B-C, 3-3-5)

### Regular Flat

- A and B corrective.
- C motive and usually near A extreme.

### Expanded Flat

- B exceeds A origin.
- C extends beyond A extreme in counter-trend direction.

### Running Flat

- B exceeds A origin.
- C fails to reach A extreme against dominant trend (trend is strong).

## Triangle Family (A-B-C-D-E, 3-3-3-3-3)

### Contracting Triangle

- Converging boundaries.
- Common continuation behavior after completion.

### Barrier Triangle

- One side approximately horizontal.
- Common chart analog: ascending/descending triangle.

### Expanded Triangle

- Diverging boundaries with growing swings.

## Combinations

### Double Three (W-X-Y)

- Two corrective structures connected by X.
- More sideways than a steep double zigzag.

### Triple Three (W-X-Y-X-Z)

- Three corrective structures connected by two X waves.
- Often prolonged consolidation and false breaks.

## Pattern Disambiguation Quick Test

1. Count legs first (ABC vs ABCDE vs WXY/WXYXZ).
2. Validate internal structure family (5-3-5, 3-3-5, 3-3-3-3-3).
3. Check boundary geometry (converging, flat side, diverging).
4. Confirm placement context in larger count.
5. Mark invalidation level and best alternate.
