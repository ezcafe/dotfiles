# Confluence and Psychology

All items in this file are guidelines (probability tools). They confirm or weaken a count but do not override hard rules. Missing alternation, a channel miss, or a psychological mismatch alone does not invalidate a structurally valid count.

## Alternation

### Wave 2 vs Wave 4 (Inside a Five-Wave Impulse)

| If Wave 2 is... | Then Wave 4 tends to be... |
|---|---|
| Simple / sharp / deeper (often zigzag, ~61.8% retrace of W1) | Complex / sideways / shallower (flat, triangle, combination, ~38.2% retrace of W3) |
| Complex / sideways | Simple / sharp |

### Wave A vs Wave B (Inside an A-B-C Correction)

| Variation | Wave A form | Wave B form | Wave C |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Flat (ABC) | Zigzag (ABC) | Five-wave countertrend |
| 2 | Zigzag (ABC) | Flat (ABC) | Five-wave countertrend |

### Alternation Rules

- Treat alternation as a confidence booster, not invalidation by itself.
- The ~61.8% (Wave 2) and ~38.2% (Wave 4) fib zones are soft context from the framework, not hard targets.
- If evaluating a full (A)-(B)-(C), check whether (C) still resolves as a five-wave countertrend leg.

## Channeling (Parallel Channels in Impulses)

### Temporary Channel (After Wave 3)

| Step | Action |
|---|---|
| 1 | Identify Waves 1, 2, and 3 |
| 2 | Draw upper line through highs of Wave 1 and Wave 3 |
| 3 | Draw parallel lower line through low of Wave 2 |
| 4 | Project Wave 4 support at the lower rail |

### Complete Channel (For Wave 4 and Wave 5)

| Condition | Action |
|---|---|
| Wave 4 reacts near lower rail | Channel becomes the complete impulse channel |
| Wave 5 target | Upper rail zone (guideline, not guaranteed endpoint) |

### Redraw Rule (When Wave 4 Misses)

| Step | Action |
|---|---|
| 1 | Connect ends of Wave 2 and Wave 4 as the new baseline |
| 2 | Draw parallel line from end of Wave 3 |
| 3 | Estimate Wave 5 from the new upper rail |

### Channel Invalidation

If price breaks the channel prematurely and continues in a way that violates the working impulse read, the channeling idea is invalid. Reassess the wave count.

## Psychology by Wave Position

### Impulse Waves (1-5)

| Wave | Crowd State | Typical Behavior | Confirmation Cue |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Low consensus; prior trend still looks intact | Choppy, overlapping sub-waves; wedge/diagonal shapes | Hard to identify in real time |
| 2 | Old-trend bias; most expect prior trend to resume | Deep retracement; misses that a new trend may be forming | Higher low holds (bullish) or lower high holds (bearish) |
| 3 | Broad recognition; trend change accepted | Almost always the largest move; strong momentum, no major interruptions; frequently extended | Higher high after higher low (bullish) confirms trend change |
| 4 | Fatigue / consolidation | Time-consuming, complex; generates false technical signals; alternates with Wave 2 in character | Chop and whipsaw; structure completion signals end |
| 5 | Final push; exhaustion | Lower volume; divergence on RSI/MACD vs Wave 3 peak; momentum underperformance | Bearish divergence (bull 5) or bullish divergence (bear 5) |

### Corrective Waves (A-B-C)

| Wave | Crowd State | Trap Type | Structural Signal |
|---|---|---|---|
| A | Exhaustion from prior five-wave leg; treated as normal pullback | Misread: most think dominant trend continues | Can be three or five waves; reflects exhaustion |
| B | False confidence; counter-trend bets pile in | Bull trap (bearish ABC) or bear trap (bullish ABC); fake breakout; can exceed prior high/low; often resembles right shoulder of H&S | Shape is highly variable; can retrace deeply or shoot fast |
| C | Decisive completion | Punishes impulse-wave participants; fast and devastating | Five waves against dominant trend; length often equals A, can extend to 1.618x A |

### Psychology Application Rules

- Psychology guides narrative; it does not override hard rules.
- Tie psychology claims to structure (subdivisions, alternation, channeling).
- If the "story" does not fit without forcing the count, reconsider the label.
- Keep bullish and bearish narrative symmetric.

## Indicator Confluence

Use indicators as confirmation tools, not as label-defining evidence.

### Checklist

| Category | Indicator | What to Check |
|---|---|---|
| Trend alignment | Moving averages (e.g. 20/50 MA) | Slope direction and crossover alignment with wave direction |
| Momentum | RSI (e.g. 14-period) | Divergence vs price at Wave 5 / Wave C; overbought/oversold at wave extremes |
| Momentum | MACD (e.g. 12/26/9) | Divergence at terminal waves; histogram momentum shift |
| Volatility | ATR (e.g. 14-period) | Normalization of swing sizes; volatility expansion/contraction confirmation |

### Integration Priority

1. Structure must be valid first.
2. Indicators confirm; they do not define Elliott labels.
3. Require at least 2-3 confluence factors before increasing confidence.
4. A single indicator alignment is insufficient for high confidence.

## Anti-Bias Safeguards

- Psychology does not override hard rules. If structure says one thing and narrative says another, trust structure.
- Do not force certainty in prolonged ranges or complex corrections.
- Keep bullish and bearish framing symmetric -- apply the same logic in both directions.
- If data is contradictory, return best two scenarios and the confirmation signals needed to resolve.
